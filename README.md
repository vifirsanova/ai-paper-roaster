# AI Paper Roaster

A friendly inter-university community where we read papers and make the world a better place (probably).

## Our Goal

To pester scientists with tricky questions, create ideas, and do research together — solo or as a group.

We read papers, criticize them — roast them like true academic fellows — and suggest improvements. The whole point is to help you do your own research.

## When

Every Saturday at 12 PM Moscow time.

We meet every week. Once a month, we invite a guest speaker from a university or tech company. You can become a speaker too.

## Where

Zoom. Link TBA.

## Seminar Plan

1. A pool of papers is announced.
2. Pick one paper.
3. Skim the paper. You have 25 minutes.
   - You get questions that help guide your reading.
   - You can use AI to help with terminology.
4. Ask the author 3 questions. You can email them later.
5. Find one weak spot and suggest an idea for improvement.

The meeting lasts 1.5 hours.
